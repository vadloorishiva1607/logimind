SAFETY AND PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY

Driver performance is tracked on three primary metrics: on-time delivery
percentage, safety score, and incident count. A safety score decline of
more than 10 points over a rolling 3-week period should trigger a
recommended performance review, regardless of whether an incident has
occurred, since declining scores often precede reportable incidents.

A single incident report, combined with any safety score decline in the
same period, should be treated as a higher-priority review candidate than
either factor alone.

Performance reviews are conducted by a Driver Manager and may result in
additional training, a documented warning, or in repeated cases, referral
toward the Driver Termination Policy process. The AI assistant's role is
limited to identifying and explaining the pattern, and recommending that a
review take place.
