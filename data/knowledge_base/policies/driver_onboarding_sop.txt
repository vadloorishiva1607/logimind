DRIVER ONBOARDING SOP

New drivers enter "onboarding" status upon signing their initial agreement
and remain in this status until all required steps are complete: background
check clearance, DOT medical certification, defensive driving certification,
and a supervised first-week ride-along.

Onboarding is expected to complete within 30 days of the start date. Drivers
exceeding 45 days in onboarding status should be flagged for Driver Manager
follow-up, as this typically indicates a missing document or scheduling
delay rather than disqualification.

Once all steps are verified, a Driver Manager updates the driver's status to
"active" in the Logistics Application. The AI assistant may report onboarding
progress but does not change onboarding status itself.
