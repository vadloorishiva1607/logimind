SETTLEMENT CALCULATION SOP

Weekly settlement is calculated as: Gross Pay minus Fuel Deduction minus
Escrow Deduction minus Other Deductions equals Net Pay.

Gross pay is determined by completed, confirmed-delivered shipments within
the settlement week, calculated per the driver's rate confirmation
agreement. Fuel deductions reflect actual fuel card transactions for the
period. Escrow deductions follow the standard weekly contribution unless a
withdrawal or adjustment applies per the Escrow Withdrawal Policy.

A settlement decrease of more than 15 percent week-over-week should be
explained by identifying which deduction category changed and by how much,
cross-referencing Fuel and Escrow agent data before presenting a conclusion
to the user. If deduction data is incomplete for the period, the explanation
should state that explicitly rather than estimating.
