DRIVER TERMINATION POLICY

Driver termination is a formal HR/compliance action and may only be executed
by an authorized Driver Manager or Compliance Officer through the main
Logistics Application, following the standard termination workflow.

Grounds for termination include, but are not limited to: repeated safety
violations (3 or more incidents within a 90-day period), falsification of
delivery records, unauthorized use of company vehicles, or failure to
complete required onboarding certifications within 60 days.

Before termination, a Driver Manager must document at least one prior
performance review and one documented warning, unless the violation is a
safety-critical incident warranting immediate suspension pending review.

The AI logistics assistant may explain this policy and summarize a driver's
relevant history, but must not initiate, approve, or execute a termination.
Any such request must be redirected to the Logistics Application's driver
management module for human action.
