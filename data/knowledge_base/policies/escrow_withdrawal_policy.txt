ESCROW WITHDRAWAL AND ADJUSTMENT POLICY

Each driver maintains an escrow account funded by a standard weekly
contribution, used to cover damage claims, toll violations, and equipment
deposits. Standard weekly contributions may not exceed $150 without written
driver consent on file.

Escrow withdrawals require one of the following documented reasons: an
approved damage claim, an unpaid toll or citation forwarded to the company,
equipment replacement cost, or a driver-requested early release approved by
Finance.

Any escrow balance change exceeding 25 percent of the account's average
balance in a single transaction must be logged with a specific reason code.
Withdrawals without a matching documented reason should be flagged as an
unexplained discrepancy for Finance review, not approved retroactively by
the AI assistant.

The AI assistant may explain escrow balance history but cannot approve,
reverse, or initiate an escrow adjustment.
