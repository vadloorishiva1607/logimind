SHIPMENT DELAY AND RISK ESCALATION SOP

A shipment is marked "delayed" when actual ETA exceeds scheduled ETA by more
than 60 minutes. Common delay reasons include traffic, weather, mandatory
driver rest breaks (DOT hours-of-service compliance), mechanical issues, and
loading/unloading dock delays.

A single delayed shipment with a known, documented cause is considered
routine and requires no escalation. A pattern of 3 or more delays for the
same driver or the same route within a 2-week period should be escalated as
a systemic risk, cross-referencing Driver and Performance data to determine
whether the pattern is route-related or driver-related.

Shipments delayed with no recorded reason code should be flagged as "cause
unconfirmed" rather than assigned a probable cause, since guessing a wrong
cause can misdirect a real investigation.
