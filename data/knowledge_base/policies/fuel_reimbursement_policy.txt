FUEL REIMBURSEMENT AND DEDUCTION POLICY

Fuel costs are deducted from a driver's weekly settlement based on actual
fuel card transactions recorded during the settlement period. Drivers are
reimbursed at the company-negotiated fuel rate, not the retail price paid,
when the two differ by more than 10 percent.

A fuel efficiency baseline of 6.5 MPG is used for standard tractor-trailer
routes. Deviations below this baseline by more than 15 percent in a given
week trigger an automatic flag for Fleet Maintenance review, as this may
indicate a mechanical issue rather than driving behavior.

Fuel deductions above $50 more than the driver's trailing 4-week average
should be treated as anomalous and are eligible for a Fuel Agent explanation
citing the specific transactions responsible.
